The platform

A buying register that protects the margin on every deal.

Most shops lose money one deal at a time — a misjudged premium here, an undocumented payout there. The register makes disciplined pricing the default and puts every transaction on paper.

Pricing

Dual pricing engine

Every item priced against melt, wholesale, and retail comps at once, with a max payout ceiling that keeps offers inside your margin rules.

Data

Live market feeds

Real-time spot prices, PCGS certification lookup, CDN Greysheet wholesale values, and recent eBay sold listings — pulled into the ticket automatically.

Control

Manager override trail

Staff can't exceed payout ceilings without a manager PIN. Every override is logged, so you see exactly who paid what and why.

Compliance

Built-in hard stops

Automatic checkpoints for regulated situations — including pre-1933 gold handling and cash transaction thresholds — before a deal can close.

Paper trail

Signed offer documents

Every accepted deal generates an offer letter PDF with customer signature, stored with photos and pricing detail for your records.

Follow-up

CRM handoff

Accepted deals flow to your CRM by webhook, so follow-up, reviews, and repeat business run without retyping a single record.

How a deal runs

Intake to signed offer in one pass.

Intake

Log the customer and item on the iPad at the counter — photos, cert numbers, and condition captured in the ticket.

Price

The register pulls live melt, wholesale bid, and sold comps, then computes an offer inside your payout rules. No guessing at the counter.

Close

Customer signs the offer document, compliance checks clear, the record archives, and the deal hands off to follow-up automatically.

About Alliance Strategic Holdings

Alliance Strategic Holdings LLC develops and licenses the Coin Appraisal Register, a subscription software platform for independent coin and collectibles shop operators, and publishes Coin Shop Mastery, an operator training course.

Revenue comes from software subscriptions, setup fees, and course sales. Our customers are US-based small business owners who buy and sell coins, bullion, and collectibles — and who want the pricing discipline and paper trail of a much larger operation without hiring one.
